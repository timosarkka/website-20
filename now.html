I'm now a dbt Certified Developer

I passed the exam! It was a bit harder than expected actually. If you're going to take it, I recommend reviewing all the fundamental concepts of dbt and then doing some free practice tests (you'll find a bunch of them through Google). Aim for about ~90% pass rate in the practice exams before taking on the real one.
