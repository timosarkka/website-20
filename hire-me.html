How I Can Help

I'm an analytics engineer with hands-on experience building modern data stacks from the ground up. If your team is drowning in messy data, struggling with slow pipelines, or missing the visibility to make good decisions — that's the kind of problem I enjoy solving.

Specifically, I can help you with:

  • Designing and building scalable data pipelines with dbt and Snowflake
  • Implementing Data Vault 2.0 modelling for robust historical data tracking
  • Migrating legacy ETL workflows to a modern, maintainable stack
  • Setting up testing, documentation, and data quality frameworks
  • Coaching your in-house team on dbt best practices
  • One-off audits of your existing dbt project or data architecture

Ways of Working

I work best in focused, async-friendly engagements. I'm not looking for micromanaged hour-tracking setups — I prefer to agree on clear outcomes and deliver them.

  • Project-based. A scoped piece of work with a clear deliverable. Best for migrations, new pipeline builds, or audits.
  • Part-time retainer. A set number of hours per week, ongoing. Good for teams that need consistent support without a full-time hire.
  • Advisory. Regular calls to review architecture decisions, unblock your team, or sanity-check direction.

I'm based in Helsinki (EET/EEST) and work primarily in English and Finnish.
